# AI Music Tube

A small Rails, Hotwire, and SQLite catalog of AI-generated and AI-assisted music. Browse by track or artist, search by title, artist, or genre, and build a private library. The official YouTube embed handles playback; the app stores no media and is not affiliated with or endorsed by YouTube or Google.

## Run locally

Use Ruby 4.0.10 (see `.ruby-version`), Bundler, and SQLite.

```sh
bundle install
bin/rails db:prepare
bin/rails db:seed
bin/rails server
```

Open <http://localhost:3000>. [About](http://localhost:3000/about) shows live artist, upload, and tag counts. [Tags](http://localhost:3000/tags) lists filters, including **Mixes**, with their catalog totals. The desktop sidebar remembers its size in this browser. No YouTube API key or login is required.

Select the play icon on a track page to load the embedded player. If an upload or browser blocks it, use **Watch on YouTube**. **History** records a play click or a direct open, not whether you listened. Create a playlist from **Playlists** or use **Save** on a track page. Likes, history, and playlists are stored in SQLite and attached to this browser by an encrypted Rails cookie. They do not sync; clearing the cookie loses access to that library.

## Catalog

Watch links use the YouTube video ID (`/watch/VIDEO_ID`) and work across independent installs. Old numeric links redirect using the original database. When moving the app, restore the SQLite database and keep the same Rails cookie encryption key; a fresh seed cannot restore numeric bookmarks or private libraries.

The seed covers 2,148 uploads from 335 artists and channels, including [Saindra’s “Queso”](https://saindra.gallery/en/discography/) and 728 entries under **Mixes**. It includes dedicated uploads for all tracks on YACHT’s *Chain Tripping*, Taryn Southern’s *I AM AI*, SKYGGE’s *Hello World*, and Frostbite Orckings’ *The Orcish Eclipse*. The catalog is curated, not a complete discography for every artist. Each upload has a note and a source on its detail page:

- **AI-generated:** the source credits AI for generating the music or a defining part such as the lead vocal. Human-written lyrics do not rule this out.
- **AI-assisted:** AI contributed to a human-led recording or composition, or the creator discloses AI use in the music without specifying the parts.
- Uploads with only a general AI disclosure and reader picks without audio-specific evidence have no public label. The source note remains on the track page.

An AI-made video, a distributor's “Auto-generated by YouTube” footer, or a sound that seems synthetic is not evidence of AI use in the audio.

Add documented uploads in [`db/seeds.rb`](db/seeds.rb) and rerun `bin/rails db:seed`. If a Topic channel has no Videos tab, search for its artist and release titles, then check the exact public upload and the AI evidence separately. Seeding updates by YouTube ID without resetting libraries. It does not remove old entries or check links on a schedule.

[Kelly Boesch’s music films](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLT26wpyuKBpMf99VVaNuCmYeUXcTOQEwJ) and the multi-channel [AI Covers of Popular Songs playlist](https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLReI40KGodv6r0WRDxNJwM1MkBcoN3Gox) are the discovery sources for [these additions](db/catalog/music_playlists.json). The source note for Moon Tunes’ [*Whiskey Blues* mix](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=knFceIUXCXU) quotes the watch page’s “How this was made” panel.

The November 25, 2026 snapshot includes all 84 videos on [Moon Tunes’ Videos tab](https://www.youtube.com/@moontunes1/videos) and all 296 mixes on [Oldies Jazz Lounge’s Videos tab](https://www.youtube.com/@OldiesJazzLounge/videos). SerpApi YouTube Music search results were matched by video ID to the paginated SerpApi YouTube Channel listings, and each upload was checked with YouTube oEmbed. Moon Tunes’ five Shorts are not counted as separate recordings. Oldies Jazz Lounge’s mixes are listed as reader picks; the channel does not give a channel-wide AI music credit.

The same snapshot includes the Videos tabs of [Persian Instrumental Music](https://www.youtube.com/@PersianCalmMusic/videos) (255 uploads), [Dark Avenue Jazz](https://www.youtube.com/@DarkAvenueJazz-x4l/videos) (10), [Soul Tapes](https://www.youtube.com/@SoulTapesboombap/videos) (164), and [SoulSweetHoney](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCZduEiGUrbMtOBa_j0CUpMA/videos) (102). SerpApi’s channel listings supplied the full upload lists; YouTube Music search provided additional matches, and YouTube oEmbed confirmed the video ID and uploader for all 531. Persian Instrumental Music [credits AI tools for its music](https://www.youtube.com/@PersianCalmMusic/about). The other three have no verified audio-specific AI credit in the sources checked.

[StarSinger artists](http://localhost:3000/artists?source=starsinger) contribute 711 of those uploads across 191 profiles. The [bundled snapshot](db/catalog/starsinger.json) matches artist and song titles from the [public directory](https://starsinger.ai/artists) to recordings on [StarSinger’s official YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@StarSinger-AI/videos), checked with YouTube oEmbed. The artist profile is the source for the publisher’s AI disclosure. Keep separate profile IDs even when names or channels coincide. Exclude promo Shorts, ambiguous credits, videos without an embed, and profiles without a matching upload.

[Most played](http://localhost:3000/?sort=most_played) ranks uploads by clicks on the embedded player’s play button or **Watch on YouTube**. Each click increments an aggregate on the track, independent of private history. The initial count includes one click per retained browser/track history entry; earlier repeat clicks weren’t recorded. Neither page visits nor YouTube statistics affect the ranking.

**Suggest an artist:** add the catalog entry on a branch in your fork, then [open a pull request on GitHub](https://github.com/skatkov/aimusictube.com/compare?expand=1). Include the YouTube video or channel link and a source for any AI music credit. Artists and About link to the same page. Suggestions submitted through the old form remain private in the local `artist_submissions` table.
